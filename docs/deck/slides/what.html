The register

AtliQ’s usual terms

liability 1× fees, payment in 30 days, mutual NDAs…

The deal

its value, deadline and which AtliQ company signs

Company and law rules

US clients sign with AtliQ Inc, everyone else with Pvt Ltd

The other documents in the deal

BAA, SOWs, subcontracts, call notes

It gives back

A risk score

0–100 with a Low / Medium / High band

A three-line summary

each line links to the finding behind it

Findings, Serious first

quote, what it means, what it could cost, how to soften it

A “Not sure” tray

anything unverified is shown, never guessed

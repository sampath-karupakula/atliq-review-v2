AtliQ Review

A contract reviewer for a company with no lawyer

It reads every incoming contract against what AtliQ has already promised, and tells the founder what matters before he signs.

AI suggests · code checks · the person decides

AtliQ is a founder-led IT services company with two entities, AtliQ Inc for US clients and AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd for everyone else, delivering from Pune. There is no in-house lawyer. Karandeep, the founder, reads and signs every contract. AtliQ Review is the tool we built to help him: it informs, it never signs, sends or edits anything.

In 2025 AtliQ promised Al Noor not to sell analytics to Gulf hotels until 14 Sep 2029. Gulf Crown’s new draft asks for exactly that, and also makes AtliQ swear it is bound by no such restriction.

The risk isn’t only bad clauses. It’s clauses that clash with what AtliQ already signed. The four live promises are Al Noor’s Gulf hospitality non-compete, Crestline’s best-price clause, CloudSpan’s partner exclusivity and Northwind’s IP assignment including PipeKit. Nobody keeps these in their head, and missing one means breaching a contract the day you sign the next.

AI suggests. Code checks.
The person decides.

The AI

Reads every clause, spots risks and clashes, explains them in plain English and drafts softer wording.

The code

Does every date, sum and exposure figure, entity and law rule, live or expired status, the score, and the word-for-word quote check.

The founder

Dismisses, accepts or pushes back, and decides. The tool never signs, sends or edits a contract.

AtliQ Review

4

This is the design principle behind everything. Language models are good at reading and explaining; they are not reliable at arithmetic or dates. So anything that is a fact or a number is code: “$3,600 a day, 50 days to reach the full fee” is computed from the deal value, and “live until 14 Sep 2029” is computed from the register. Sign, approve, send, e-sign and auto-redline were deliberately never built.

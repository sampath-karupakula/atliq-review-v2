Contracts rarely travel alone. A healthcare deal has an MSA, a BAA, an SOW and subcontracts, and the risk often sits in the gaps between them. The deal page lists what’s required, flags conflicts between documents, and blocks patient data from moving until the BAA is signed. “Can I offer this?” is for the sales call itself: it checks a price or a scope against the register and the rate card as you type. The mirror check turns the tables: when AtliQ sends its own paper to a freelancer or agency, it asks whether AtliQ would accept the same terms from a client.

# AtliQ Review: presentation deck

14 slides (16:9) for presenting the project: the problem, how a review works, the Gulf Crown example, the trust and
privacy rules, the register, the workflow, deals, evaluation results, the build, and what's next. Every slide has
speaker notes.

- **Live deck:** <https://claude.ai/artifact/KUjRaiU9cYR31N9FwmTVss>. Present it, or export it as PowerPoint or PDF,
  from its Share menu. It is private until shared.
- **This folder** is the deck's source, kept in the repo so the content is versioned with the code:
  `deck.json` (title, slide order, sections, typefaces) and one `slides/<id>.html` per slide in the Slides format.

Figures on the slides come from the data and the evaluation run of October 2026 (README → Evaluation). The name, email
and bank number on the privacy slide are made up for illustration. If a figure changes, update the slide here and in
the live deck.

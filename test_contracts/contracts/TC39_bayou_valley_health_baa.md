# BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

This Business Associate Agreement ("**BAA**") is made between **Bayou Valley Health System Inc.**, a Louisiana nonprofit corporation with its office at 5000 Hennessy Boulevard, Baton Rouge, Louisiana 70808, USA ("**Covered Entity**"), and **ATLIQ INC.**, a Delaware corporation with its office at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540, USA ("**Business Associate**"), and supplements the Master Services Agreement between them (the "**MSA**").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Terms used but not defined in this BAA have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules (45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164).

1.2 "**PHI**" means protected health information created, received, maintained or transmitted by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity.

1.3 "**Subcontractor**" means any person or entity, including any Affiliate of Business Associate, to whom Business Associate delegates a function, activity or service involving PHI.

## 2. PERMITTED USES

2.1 Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only to perform the services under the MSA and as required by law.

## 3. SAFEGUARDS AND REPORTING

3.1 Business Associate shall implement the safeguards required by the HIPAA Security Rule and report any breach of unsecured PHI within five (5) business days of discovery.

## 4. LOCATION OF PHI; SUBCONTRACTORS

4.1 Business Associate shall not, and shall ensure that no Subcontractor shall, access, store or process PHI from any location outside the United States, whether directly or by remote access.

4.2 Business Associate shall not engage any Subcontractor without Covered Entity's prior written approval. Each approved Subcontractor shall be organised under the laws of a State of the United States, and all its personnel performing services shall be located in the United States.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 This BAA lasts as long as the MSA. On termination Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI.

## 6. GOVERNING LAW

6.1 This BAA is governed by the laws of the State of Louisiana and applicable federal law.
